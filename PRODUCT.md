# Product

<!-- impeccable:product-schema 1 -->

## Platform

web

## Stack

delegated: static HTML/CSS/JS in a single page, no build step. Chosen because a home baker needs a page that can be hosted anywhere for free (GitHub Pages, Netlify) and edited by hand.

## Users

Neighbors, friends-of-friends and local customers of a home baker who hear about Rest Bakery by word of mouth or social media. They arrive on a phone, want to see what is baked, and want an easy way to place an order or ask for a custom bake.

## Product Purpose

Rest Bakery is a home bakery: small-batch baking from a home kitchen, sold by order rather than from a storefront. The landing page exists to make people excited about the bakes and turn that excitement into an order inquiry. Success = visitors send an order request.

## Positioning

A single home baker, not a shop: everything is made to order, in small batches, by one person. Open: the specific signature items, the area served, and the pickup/delivery arrangement are not confirmed yet.

## Operating Context

Orders come in by message or form; the baker confirms by hand and arranges pickup. No online payments, no fixed hours storefront.

## Capabilities and Constraints

- Order inquiry is the primary action (no checkout).
- Undecided and must not be invented as fact: menu items, prices, service area, lead times, contact handles, address.

## Brand Commitments

- Name: Rest Bakery.
- Owner asked for the page to feel "fun and exciting", then rejected a playful sprinkle-themed design as "too childish" and chose a clean, modern bakery look. Energy should come from confident type, colour and motion, never kid-party motifs.
- Owner referenced a 99designs design (profiles/3208811/designs/1194989) that could not be viewed from this session; owner chose to let the design direction be decided freely.

## Evidence on Hand

None. No photos, testimonials, reviews, press, or menu. Future work must not fabricate testimonials, ratings, prices or customer counts. Any illustrative menu content is placeholder and must be listed for replacement.

## Product Principles

1. Make people hungry first, then make ordering effortless.
2. Feel like a person, not a chain: warm, personal voice.
3. Honest about the small-batch, made-to-order nature.
4. Phone-first: most visitors arrive from a shared link.
