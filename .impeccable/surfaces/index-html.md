# Landing page (index.html)

Scope: Rest Bakery home page. Persuade. Audience: local followers and friends arriving from Instagram on a phone. Action: request a bake for Saturday pickup. Proof: the baker's own Instagram photos; no prices, ratings or testimonials.

History: sprinkle jar (rejected, "too childish"), clean cobalt modern (superseded), now pinned by the owner to the Mooty Dribbble reference.

## Direction contract

THESIS: A product-picker storefront in the Mooty mould: the first viewport is a real bake photo beside a working menu picker. Refuses generic hero-plus-cards.

OWN-WORLD: Blush ground, pink and peach rounded panels, deep maroon ink and actions, Leckerli One script wordmark with white outline, Bricolage Grotesque 800 headlines, round-thumb option cards with radio dots, dashed pickup pill.

STORY: Visitor sees this week's bake, flips between bakes, picks quantity, taps "Request this bake", lands in a prefilled form, sends by email.

FIRST VIEWPORT: Script logo overlapping the photo's corner, caps nav right; photo panel left (~55%); pink panel right with bake name, description, next-Saturday pill, 2x2 option cards, quantity and maroon CTA.

FORM: Brief-pinned (Mooty reference), roll overridden by the owner. seed:db2f540f6c6ea8d2. Signature interaction: option cards swap the hero photo and copy.

FINISH: unreviewed and undocumented is unfinished; this build ends with the finish review, the verdict, DESIGN.md, and every shipping raster carrying its provenance
