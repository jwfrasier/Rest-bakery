The menu

A sample of what comes out of the oven. Ask what's baking this week, or request something of your own.

  • Celebration cakes Tall vanilla, chocolate or lemon layers with whipped buttercream. Message on top, candles on request.
  • Chocolate chip cookies Crisp edges, soft middles, plenty of chocolate. By the dozen.
  • Cinnamon rolls Soft swirls of brown sugar and cinnamon under a cream cheese glaze.
  • Country sourdough Long, slow ferment, crackly crust, open crumb.
  • Cupcakes Your choice of flavor, frosted generously. Great for parties.
  • Fudgy brownies Shiny crackle top, dense chocolate center.

How ordering works

  1. 1

    Send a request

    Tell me what you'd like, how many and the date you need it. Add flavors, allergies or the occasion.

  2. 2

    I confirm the details

    I'll reply to confirm availability, the price and when it will be ready. Nothing is charged until we agree.

  3. 3

    Baked fresh, just for you

    Your order is mixed and baked in a small batch close to pickup, then boxed and ready to go.
